Defend Your Build: 3-Minute
Engineering Walkthrough (Video)
Create a video on what you've built
Record a 4 minute video explaining the Agent Platform assignment you just submitted. This is not a demo - we've already run your tests. We want to hear how you think.
Cover these four things, in whatever order works for you:
1. ﻿﻿﻿What you shipped. Which modules you completed, which you skipped, and how you decided what to work on first.
2. ﻿﻿﻿One decision you're proud of. Pick a single implementation choice - a retry strategy, how you ranked memory, how you enforced permission scopes, how you made the winner deterministic — and explain why you did it that way and what you rejected.
3. ﻿﻿﻿One thing that fought you. A test you couldn't pass, a contract that was ambiguous, or a bug that cost you time.
Tell us what was happening and how you attacked it.
4. ﻿﻿﻿What breaks in production. Point at something in your owr code that would not survive real enterprise traffic, and say what you'd change.
Upload the video as an accessible Google Drive link, Loom link or an unlisted YouTube link, and paste the link to the video in the submission area.
Requirements:
* ﻿﻿Show your face and your screen. Talking over your code is encouraged; slides are not needed.
* ﻿﻿Reference specific files, functions, or test names. Generic descriptions of "agentic best practices" score zero.
* ﻿﻿Hard limit: 4 minutes. We stop watching at 4:00. Being concise is part of the evaluation.
* ﻿﻿Unedited single take is fine and preferred. Presentation polish is not what we're scoring.
